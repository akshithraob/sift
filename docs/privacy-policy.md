# Leakproof Privacy Policy

Version 1 · Effective 18 October 2026

Leakproof is published by **Akshith Rao B**. This policy explains what the app does with your information, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

## The short version

- Everything happens on your phone. There is no account, no server and no cloud copy.
- The app has no network permission, so it cannot send your data anywhere.
- There are no ads, analytics, crash reporters or trackers.
- You can see, correct, export or delete everything the app holds, and you can withdraw your consent at any time.

## What the app handles

**Things you give it**
- Screenshots of your UPI app's Autopay or Mandates screen, and bank statements (PDF or CSV), that you choose. Text is read on your phone by an on-device text recognizer. The app works on a temporary copy that is deleted as soon as it has been read.
- A PDF password, if your statement is protected. It is used only in memory to open the file and is never saved or logged.
- Details you type in: subscription names, amounts, renewal dates, notes, free-trial end dates, and an optional monthly budget.

**What it saves (in an encrypted database on your phone)**
- Merchant name, amount, how often it is charged, next renewal date, payment type (UPI, e-NACH, standing instruction, card), where it came from, status, the last 4 digits of the account if a screenshot or statement shows them, the mandate limit if shown, your notes and any trial end date.
- Your settings: app lock, reminders, budget, and a record that you gave consent.

**What it does not save**
- Full account or card numbers, UPI IDs, the text of your screenshots or statements, or any transaction that is not a recurring payment.

## Why the app uses this information

Only to do what you asked: list your recurring payments, remind you before they renew or a free trial ends, point out overlapping services and unusually high mandate limits, track your budget, and show you how to cancel. Nothing is used for advertising, profiling or sold to anyone.

## Where it is kept and how it is protected

- Stored only on your phone, in a database encrypted with AES-256. The key is protected by the Android Keystore.
- The app can require your fingerprint, face or screen lock to open, and locks again when you leave it.
- Screenshots and screen recording of the app are blocked.
- Android's own cloud backup and device-transfer are switched off for this app.

## Who it is shared with

Nobody. Leakproof has no network permission and does not contain any service that receives your data. The text recognizer runs on your phone using a model bundled with the app.

## Backups and exports you create

- **Encrypted backup:** protected by a passphrase you choose. We never see or store the passphrase and cannot recover it. If you forget it, the backup cannot be opened.
- **CSV export:** a plain, unencrypted list of your subscriptions. Anyone who gets the file can read it, so store or share it carefully.
- These files are saved where you choose. Deleting your data in the app does not delete files you saved elsewhere.

## Notifications

Reminders are created on your phone. On the lock screen they show only a generic message; details appear after you unlock.

## How long it is kept

Until you remove it: delete a single subscription, use **Delete all my data** in Settings (this erases the database and its encryption key), or uninstall the app.

## Your rights under the DPDP Act

- **Access:** everything held is visible in the app, and you can export it.
- **Correction:** edit any subscription.
- **Erasure:** delete one entry or everything.
- **Withdraw consent:** at any time, as easily as you gave it, in Settings → **Delete all my data & withdraw consent**. Withdrawing erases your data from the app.
- **Grievance:** write to the contact below. We will reply within 30 days.
- If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

## Children

Leakproof is not meant for anyone under 18.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects you, the app will show you the new version and ask for your consent again before you continue.
